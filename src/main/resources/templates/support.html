Customer Support

How do I track my order?
Once your order ships, we'll notify you with tracking details. You can also contact us anytime with your Order ID.

We currently accept Cash on Delivery (COD). Online payment options will be available soon.
How long does delivery take?
Orders are typically delivered within 5–7 business days, depending on your location.
Can I cancel my order?
Orders can be cancelled before they are shipped. Contact us as soon as possible at support@alvirajewellery.com.
Still need help?
